What you get

One device per station, with sensors that behave like Home Assistant expects: proper device classes, long-term statistics and translations.

CPM and dose rate

Counts per minute plus µSv/h, from the station itself or calculated with a conversion factor you can tune to the tube.

Radiation alert

A safety binary sensor that turns on at or above a CPM threshold you set. Hook it up to any notification.

Online check

Know when a station stops reporting, including your own counter at home.

Location built in

Coordinates, place name and counter model as attributes and on the device page.

Keeps working

A failed poll keeps the last good reading. Diagnostic sensors show poll health, and diagnostics redact your password.

Plays nice

radmon.org is run by volunteers. Radmon polls gently and identifies itself, so the service stays up for everyone.

Owner mode or public mode

Enter a password and you're in owner mode. Leave it empty for public mode. You can switch later with Reconfigure.

Owner mode

  • For your own station, with its data-sending password
  • Uses radmon.org's official lastreading API, which needs the password since May 2026
  • Your station's own µSv/h value
  • Every 5 minutes by default, down to 1 minute

Public mode

  • For any station, just the name
  • Reads the small public station page
  • µSv/h calculated from CPM (default factor 0.0057, SBM-20)
  • Every 30 minutes by default, minimum 15, to go easy on radmon.org

Entities you get

One device per station (Radmon lImbus).

Act on it

Get a push notification when the alert turns on, or when your own counter stops uploading. Long-term statistics make a 7-day CPM graph a one-card job.

Radmon is not a safety device. Readings come from volunteers' hardware and can be late or wrong.

triggers:
  - trigger: state
    entity_id: binary_sensor.radmon_limbus_radiation_alert
    to: "on"
actions:
  - action: notify.mobile_app_your_phone
    data:
      title: "☢️ Radiation alert"
      message: "{{ states('sensor.radmon_limbus_cpm') }} CPM"

Installation

HACS, a restart, and a station name.

HACS

  1. 1. Click Open in HACS, or add https://github.com/DonTranQuiL/HA-Radmon as a custom repository (category Integration).
  2. 2. Download Radmon and restart Home Assistant.
  3. 3. Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → Radmon, then enter a station name from the station list.

Upgrading from 1.x

Existing entries migrate to public mode automatically and keep their entity IDs and history. Delete the old custom_components/ha-radmon folder after updating.

Full docs, dashboard YAML and troubleshooting are in the README.
